Salad

Description

This is a light refreshing side dish to accompany your main course. For salad dressing, choose your favorite vinaigrette or a simple olive oil and red wine vinegar blend.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups romaine lettuce that is torn, washed, and dried
  • 1 cup torn radicchio
  • 1 cup torn green leaf lettuce
  • 1 onion sliced into rings
  • 12 cherry tomatoes
  • 4 ounces feta cheese crumbled

Steps

  1. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl.
  2. Toss with your chosen salad dressing.
