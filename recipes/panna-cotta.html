Panna Cotta

Description

This is a traditional Italian dessert to finish off your meal.

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup skim milk
  • 1 0.25 ounce package unflavored gelatin
  • 2 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Pour milk into a small bowl and sprinkle gelatin powder over the milk. Stir until combined. Set aside.
  3. Heat heavy cream in a saucepan over medium heat until it begins to simmer. Remove from heat and stir in sugar and vanilla extract.
  4. Over medium heat, stir heavy cream and sugar together in a saucepan. Bring to a boil.
  5. Immediately stir gelatin mixture into boiling cream mixture. Stir until completely dissolved. Cook and stir for 1 minute.
  6. Remove pan from heat and stir in the vanilla extract.
  7. Pour mixture into 6 individual ramekins or custard cups. Let cool, uncovered, about 20 minutes.
  8. Cover ramekins with plastic wrap and refrigerate until set, at least 4 hours or overnight.
  9. If desired, serve with berries or a drizzle of caramel sauce.
